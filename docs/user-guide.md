# User guide

A short guide to the everyday work. Every page has its own help text; this explains the order things happen in.

## Roles

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| School Administrator | Setup, users and roles, pupil records, fee structure |
| Bursar / Finance Officer | Billing, payments and receipts, discounts requests, expenses, owner funds, payroll preparation, carry-forward, preparing term close |
| Head Teacher | Approves discounts, payment reversals, expenses, payroll and term close |
| Proprietor | Reads everything financial, approves expenses and payroll |
| Auditor | Reads everything, changes nothing |

An administrator can change what each role may do under Users & roles. Whoever enters something can never approve it themselves.

## Start of a term

1. **Setup > Years and terms**: make sure the term exists with the right dates.
2. **Fee structure**: choose the term and class, then "Edit fees", or "Copy from another term" and adjust.
3. **Pupils**: register new pupils (the pupil ID is issued automatically). Change class or status from the pupil's page; only Active pupils are billed.
4. **Charges > Bill a class**: choose the term and class, "Preview charges", untick optional items a pupil does not take, enter any discount with a reason, then post. Discounts wait for the head teacher.

## During the term

- **Record payment**: find the pupil, put the amounts against the fee items being paid ("Fill oldest first" helps), choose the method and reference, then save. Print the receipt. A mistake is put right by "Request reversal" on the receipt, which the head teacher approves; receipts are never edited or deleted.
- **Expenses**: "Add expense" with a funding source. It counts once approved.
- **Owner funds**: record money the proprietor puts in. It is funding, not fees income.
- **Payroll**: prepare a month, check it, submit it. Approval posts the salary cost. Payroll posting is switched off until the rates are checked (see build notes).
- **Arrears list** and **Pupil statements**: who owes what, printable and exportable.
- **Approvals**: the head teacher's queue.

## End of term

1. **Term close**: clear anything listed under "Settle these before closing", read the warnings, then "Prepare close snapshot".
2. The head teacher opens the snapshot and approves it. The term is then closed and the snapshot frozen.
3. **Carry forward**: from the closed term to the next one. Each unpaid balance becomes an Arrears B/F charge in the new term.

## Printing

Receipts, statements and payslips print on A4. The arrears list and reports print in landscape. Use the Print button on the page.
